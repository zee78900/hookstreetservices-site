Where the money leaks

  • Missed claim windows: two damage incidents last quarter were photographed but never filed; the platform’s filing window closed at 14 days. Illustrative cost: roughly $700 walked away.
  • Payouts nobody checks: nobody confirms the payouts match the bookings. The first real check on a business like this typically turns up cleaning-fee mistakes and unclaimed damage credits worth a few hundred dollars a month.
  • Vendor bills nobody checks: the pool service billed 13 visits across a 12-week stretch. Nobody caught it, because no one owns that loop.
