Privacy Policy

Effective June 1, 2026

Who we are

Hook Street Capital LLC ("Hook Street," "we," "us") operates a private, internal financial dashboard used by the company's owner to view his own business and personal accounts. This policy explains what information we access, how we use it, and how we protect it — with particular attention to financial account data accessed through Plaid Inc. ("Plaid").

What information we access

When the account owner connects a financial institution through Plaid, we access only data for accounts the owner himself links and authorizes, which may include:

  • Account balances (current and available);
  • Account and routing numbers and account names/types, used to label and de-duplicate accounts;
  • Basic institution and account metadata.

We do not collect data about any third party. The end user is the account owner himself.

How we use it

  • To display a consolidated, read-only view of the owner's own balances inside a private, access-gated dashboard for personal and business cash-flow management.
  • That is the only purpose. We do not initiate payments or move money through Plaid, do not lend, and do not use the data for advertising, profiling, or any unrelated purpose.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your financial data with third parties.
  • We do not use the data for marketing.
  • We do not initiate transfers or payments.

Plaid

We use Plaid to connect to financial institutions and retrieve the data described above. By linking an account you also agree to Plaid's End User Privacy Policy. Plaid's handling of data is governed by its own policy.

How we protect it

  • The dashboard is gated behind authenticated login (Google single sign-on via Cloudflare Access); only the authorized owner can view it.
  • Access credentials/tokens returned by Plaid are stored encrypted in server-side infrastructure and are never exposed to the browser or to any public page.
  • Data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Data retention & your choices

The owner can disconnect any linked institution at any time, which revokes our ongoing access. On request, stored access tokens and cached balances are deleted. Because the system serves a single owner reviewing his own accounts, we retain only what is needed to show current balances.
