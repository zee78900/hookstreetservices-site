Privacy Policy

Last updated July 16, 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy explains how Hook Street Capital LLC ("Hook Street," "we," "us") handles the information you share through our public services: Hook Street Services (real-estate underwriting, business capital and receivables reviews, and AI advisory) and Sommerset Hills Villas (our short-term villa rentals). It applies to the forms on these sites, including the secure document-intake form and any booking or inquiry you send us.

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to send us. Depending on how you reach out, that may include:

  • Contact and inquiry details: your name, email, phone, company, and whatever you write in a message.
  • Documents you upload: for a Deal Snapshot or a capital and receivables review, the files you send, such as offering memoranda, rent rolls, financial statements, bank statements, invoices, and profit-and-loss reports.
  • Villa booking details: for a Sommerset Hills inquiry or reservation, your name, email, phone, requested dates, and group size.

We do not buy information about you from outside sources.

How we use it

  • To respond to your inquiry and answer your question.
  • To review your deal or financials and deliver the engagement you asked for.
  • To manage a villa booking or reservation you request.

That is all. We do not use your information for advertising or profiling, and we do not add you to marketing lists without your say-so.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.
  • We do not share it with third parties, except the service providers we need to deliver what you asked for, such as the booking or payment platform that processes a villa reservation. Those providers handle your data only to perform that task.
  • We do not initiate payments or move money based on documents you send us.

How we protect it

  • Documents and form submissions travel over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.
  • Uploaded files are stored confidentially, not emailed around and not dropped in a shared folder.
  • Access is limited to Sam, who reviews what you send.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as needed to answer you, deliver the engagement, or manage your booking. Uploaded files are held in temporary secure storage and expire automatically. If you want us to delete something you sent, just ask and we will.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: info@hookstreetservices.com.

We may update this policy as our services change. Last updated: July 16, 2026.
