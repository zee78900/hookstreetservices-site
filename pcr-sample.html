Sample Owner Brief: a five-holding review

Prepared for: Meridian Family Holdings (fictional) As of: 2026-07-20 Holdings in scope: 5 of 5 Prepared by: Sam Treitel, Hook Street
What this is

A factual, sourced picture of what you own and how it is actually doing.

This brief reconstructs the current factual position of five private holdings: what was committed and contributed, what has actually come back, what was originally expected and by when, what has really happened, the latest reported value with its source and date, what is still owed or coming, what is missing, and the exact questions to send your sponsors and professionals. Every figure carries its source and as-of date. Where something cannot be sourced, it is marked unknown, not estimated.

This brief does

  • Organize your own supplied documents
  • Record reported values with source and date
  • Calculate cash in, cash out, and return % on your own reported figures
  • Compare actual events to the original written expectations
  • Flag stale, missing, or at-risk information
  • Prepare factual questions for your professionals

This brief does not

  • Recommend buying, selling, holding, or allocating
  • Select securities or manage money
  • Give an independent fair-value opinion
  • Raise capital or place investments
  • Charge on assets, performance, or transactions
  • Replace your accountant, attorney, or adviser

Here is what your money did: of the $1,235,000 you put in, $160,650 (13%, about +3.5%/yr) has actually come back in cash. Counting the latest reported values, the five holdings are marked at $1,394,800, which would be about 1.26× your money (roughly +26% total, +7%/yr blended). That step up from 13% in cash to 26% on paper is entirely unrealized and unconfirmed: two values are more than a year stale, one is a 409A option-pricing basis (not fair value), and $150,000 of note principal is past due and at risk. In confirmed cash you are at +13%; the rest depends on those marks turning real. Send-ready questions

The questions worth asking now

Consolidated from the holdings above, grouped by who should answer. Send these as written.

  1. Harborview borrower / your attorney: Is there a signed extension on the matured note? What default-rate interest has accrued since 2024-09? Is the collateral and lien confirmed, and is collection action warranted? (most time-sensitive; principal at risk)
  2. Northline company / your attorney: Why have year-2 distributions not begun? Provide the document for any personal guarantee, or confirm none exists. Is a dilutive raise planned? (unconfirmed obligation)
  3. Cedar Ridge sponsor: Did the planned 2024 refinance close? If not, why, and does it change the 2026 exit? Send the 2024 K-1 and the latest statement. (stale + missing)
  4. Ashford fund administrator: Timing and probability of the remaining $45,000 call? Is the latest distribution reinvested or paid? When does the fee step-down begin? (coming liquidity need)
  5. Custodian: What sweep rate is idle cash earning, and is a higher-yield option available in-account? (efficiency check)
