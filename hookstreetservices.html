Questions

Frequently
Asked

Everything you need to know before your trip. Don't see your question? Reach out directly.

How do I check availability and book?
+

The easiest way to check real-time availability and complete a booking is through our Airbnb listings — 9332 Sommereset Hills and 9312 Sommereset Hills. If you're interested in another property from our network or have a custom request, submit an inquiry and we'll get back to you promptly.

What's included with the villa rental?
+

All villas include fully equipped kitchens, linens and towels, resort amenity access (pools, lazy river, slides, fitness centers, playgrounds, etc.), and parking. Specific inclusions vary by property — please review the listing for full details. Add-on services like grocery provisioning, airport transfers, and private chef are available on request.

Can I request a villa in a specific resort community?
+

Absolutely. We have properties across ChampionsGate, Reunion, Eden Gardens, Windsor Hills, Solara, Emerald Island, Solterra, and more. Submit a request through our inquiry form and tell us your preferred community, group size, and dates and we'll find the best match.

How far are the villas from Disney and other parks?
+

Most of our resort communities are approximately 15–25 minutes from Walt Disney World, Universal Studios, and LEGOLAND. Drive times can be longer during peak traffic periods — we recommend building in a buffer, especially for morning rope-drop visits. Our concierge team can advise on the best routes and timing.

Do you offer airport transportation?
+

Yes — we provide private transportation from Orlando International (MCO), Orlando Sanford (SFB), JFK, LaGuardia (LGA), and Newark (EWR). Group-sized vehicles are available. Contact us in advance to schedule a pickup and get a quote.

What is your cancellation policy?
+

Cancellation policies are managed through the booking platform. For reservations made via Airbnb, the policy is displayed on the listing before you confirm your booking. For custom or direct bookings, our team will provide the applicable terms at time of reservation.

Is the contact form the right place for booking questions?
+

For availability and pricing on our flagship Airbnb listings, head directly to the listing pages where you can see live availability. Use the contact form or inquiry form for custom requests, service add-ons, questions about our villa network, or anything else you'd like to discuss before committing to a booking.
