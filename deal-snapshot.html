RE Deal Snapshot

A 24-hour independent underwriting read on a real-estate deal you're considering. Institutional-grade. Flat fee.

You found a deal. The broker's package looks good. You don't have time to underwrite it from scratch, but you can't write the LOI on gut alone. Send it, by tomorrow you'll know what's load-bearing, what's hidden, and what the return actually looks like once the broker's assumptions get stress-tested.

What you get back

  • One-page snapshot: go / maybe / walk, with the three reasons that drove it
  • Underwriting waterfall: purchase, debt service, capex, NOI, cash-on-cash, IRR, hold-period breakeven (real numbers, not the broker's)
  • Sensitivity table: your return if rents miss 5% / 10%, cap-rate softens 50 / 100 bps, refi rate rises 1 pt
  • The three hidden risks: capex you're not pricing, op-ex normalization gaps, market signals the broker glossed
  • Comp pull: 3 to 5 recent comparable transactions where available, with deltas
  • Exit math: base / bull / bear hold and refi scenarios
  • 30-minute review call after delivery to walk you through it

Who this is for

Operators & sponsors writing $1M to $25M LOIs who need a second pair of eyes before signing.

Brokers & capital sources who need a quick second opinion before a referral.
