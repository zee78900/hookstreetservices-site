Ask on WhatsApp

How does your service work?

+

Fill out our inquiry form with your dates, group size, and preferences. Once we receive your inquiry and a service deposit, we begin sourcing properties. We handle all negotiation and coordination — you just approve and book.

What is the service deposit for?

+

The deposit is a non-refundable fee covering the time and work involved in researching and coordinating your booking. It's paid before we begin your search and is separate from the actual rental cost. Full details are sent after you submit the form.

Why does the Airbnb total look higher than the nightly rate?

+

Airbnb automatically adds a guest service fee (~14%) and local taxes on top of the base nightly rate. These are set by Airbnb and completely outside our control. Our cost estimator above shows the full breakdown so there are no surprises.

Is pool heating included?

+

Pool heating is not included in the base rate. It's available as an add-on ranging from approximately $33–$75/night depending on the size of the home and pool. For a nearly 3-week Pesach stay, the electricity draw is significant and must be covered separately.

What additional services can you arrange?

+

We coordinate kitchen kashering, kosher rental items, extra refrigerators or freezers, tables and chairs, pack-n-plays, and catering referrals. These are quoted separately — just note your needs in the inquiry form.

What is the minimum age to rent?

+

Most resort communities require the primary renter to be 25 or older. If you're between 21–24, mention it in your inquiry and we'll advise on available options.

Can you find homes near a minyan or kosher store?

+

Yes — a priority for Pesach and Yom Tov groups. Several communities in the Reunion and ChampionsGate corridors have organized minyanim during Yom Tov season. Specify this in your inquiry and we'll make it a search requirement.
