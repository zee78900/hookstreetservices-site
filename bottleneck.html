What you leave with

A short, sharp written document. Not a deck.

  1. Executive summary — the single root problem creating the symptoms, and the headline recommendation. The "if you read nothing else" paragraph.
  2. The top 3 bottlenecks — each named plainly: what it is, where it's getting stuck, and who or what it depends on.
  3. What each is costing — time lost, cash tied up or leaking, revenue not captured, or how much runs only through you.
  4. A 30-day action plan — the fix-the-right-one-first sequence: concrete moves, each with an owner and a "done looks like."
  5. A 90-day roadmap — what to systematize next, in order, once the 30-day wins land.
  6. Optional: a build scope — only if you'd want it built: what Hook Street would set up, and a price band. The review stands on its own either way.
Who it fits

  • Operational chaos: things fall through the cracks in cash, follow-up, reporting, or delegation
