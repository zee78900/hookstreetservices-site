Scrapboard

a cork-board in your pocket that organizes itself
Open the link → jot → done.
That's the whole thing. Here's a little more:
1

Open & sign in

Tap Sign in with Google — the account you already have. On first sign-in it quietly makes one spreadsheet called My Scrapbook in your Google Drive. We never see it.

2

Pick what it's for

Event? Trip? Shopping? Tap a starter — or "just start jotting" for a blank board. You can change it later.

3

Jot a scrap

Type a short note (a sticky-note's worth) and hit Pin. It lands on the wall and auto-colors itself by topic — no tagging needed.

4

Find & finish

Tap a color chip to show just that topic. Tap a scrap to cross it off (it fades into the Done drawer). Switch Wall ⇄ Heatmap to see what's hot — bigger tiles = more urgent.

Good to know

Short beats long — the wall stays readable because scraps are small on purpose.
Wrong color? It'll be tap-to-recolor soon; for now the topic follows the words you use.
It's a real Google Sheet — open it in Sheets anytime; everything stays readable.
No internet? Keep jotting — it saves on your phone and syncs when you're back.

Your notes live in your own Google Drive. We host a page, not your data — with drive.file access we can only ever touch the one sheet this app creates, nothing else in your Drive.
