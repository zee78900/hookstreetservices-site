Scrapboard

a cork-board in your pocket that organizes itself
Open the link → jot → done.
That's the whole thing. Here's a little more:
1

Open & start jotting

Open the link and start — your board saves privately on this phone, no account needed. Google sign-in is coming: once it's on, one tap makes a spreadsheet called My Scrapbook in your Google Drive and your board lives there. We never see it.

2

Pick what it's for

Event? Trip? Shopping? Tap a starter — or "just start jotting" for a blank board. You can change it later.

3

Jot a scrap

Type a short note (a sticky-note's worth) and hit Pin. It lands on the wall and auto-colors itself by topic — no tagging needed.

4

Find & finish

Tap a color chip to show just that topic. Tap a scrap to cross it off (it fades into the Done drawer). Switch Wall ⇄ Heatmap to see what's hot — bigger tiles = more urgent.

5

Change a scrap's color

Wrong color? Long-press a scrap — or tap the little ● dot in its corner — and the 10-color palette pops up. Tap a color and it recolors instantly. Your pick is remembered and always beats the auto-color.

Good to know

Short beats long — the wall stays readable because scraps are small on purpose.
Wrong color? Long-press a scrap (or tap its ● dot) to open the palette and pick any of the 10 colors — your choice sticks. New scraps still auto-color by the words you use.
