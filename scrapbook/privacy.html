Scrapboard — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026
The short version: Your notes live in a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive. We do not store, copy, or read your board on our servers. We host a web page; Google hosts your data.

What we access, and why

When you sign in with Google, Scrapboard requests the drive.file scope plus your basic profile (name, email). drive.file is per-file access — it lets the app create and edit only the one spreadsheet it creates for you ("My Scrapboard"). It cannot see, list, or touch any other file in your Google Drive. That is the entire access footprint.

What we store

  • On your device: your board (and, before you sign in, any scraps you jot) are saved in your browser's local storage so the app works offline.
  • In your Google Drive: your scraps, in your "My Scrapboard" spreadsheet, which you own and can open, edit, or delete at any time.
  • On our servers: nothing about your board's contents. If you use paid AI features (later), the specific scrap text you ask us to process passes through our service transiently to return a result and is not retained.

Your control

You can revoke Scrapboard's access anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the "My Scrapboard" sheet in your Drive deletes your data. Clearing your browser storage removes the local copy.

Sharing & third parties

We do not sell or share your data. Google's handling of your Drive data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Scrapboard's use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
