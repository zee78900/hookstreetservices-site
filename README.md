# hookstreetservices-site

Hook Street Services public website (HTML, CSS, JS) deployed via Netlify.

## Pages

- `index.html` - dual-door brand landing: "Stay With Us" (villas) and "Hire Us" (advisory & systems). Redirects legacy `/#properties`-style anchors to the new pages.
- `villas.html` - luxury villa rental funnel for Orlando/ChampionsGate stays (properties, communities, trip estimator, FAQ, inquiry).
- `advisory.html` - advisory & systems hub: six audience cards linking to Deal Snapshot, Business Capital, and Business Systems.
- `deal-snapshot.html` - RE Deal Snapshot offer ($2,500 flat, 24h turnaround) with Stripe pay-now link.
- `capital.html` - Business Capital & Receivables secure intake (uploads to Cloudflare vault via ops-api).
- `systems.html` - pilot offers for Business Systems, Revenue Loop Sprint, and Youth Money Map.
- `privacy.html` - privacy policy.
